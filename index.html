🚛 Semi-trucks

Buy a semi-truck (150,000) for one of your 15 slots. Each free truck gets 4 contract offers across Europe: pick one and it drives. When it arrives, the whole contract is paid to your money. Longer routes pay more per second. A truck earns back its price in about 1.5 hours. Trips only move while the game is open.

🤖 Logistics agent: turn it on and it picks a random contract for any waiting truck 5 seconds after the offers appear. It charges 2% of that contract's profit, taken from your money the moment it picks. It waits if you can't afford the fee.

🛢️ Crude oil tankers

Buy an Aframax tanker (75 scoin) for one of your 10 slots. Each free tanker gets 4 contract offers: pick one and the tanker sails. When it arrives, the whole contract is paid to your money. Then it gets 4 new offers. Longer routes pay more per second, and the two ocean crossings pay the most. A tanker earns back its price in about 3 hours. Voyages only move while the game is open.
